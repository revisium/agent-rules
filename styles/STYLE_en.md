# English writing style

Apply to outputs whose selected language is English. Read STYLE.md for common
rules. AGENTS.md selects the language; this file does not change other outputs.

## Tone and translation

Write documentation in natural English with a neutral professional tone. Meaning
matters more than a literal translation of a draft in another language.

Use active verbs: "the system saves" rather than "the saving operation is performed".
Avoid bureaucratic introductions such as "within the framework of this document",
"it should be noted", "this approach allows" and "at the present time".

## Headings and connected prose

Headings name their subject. "What the decision changes" is more precise than
"Consequences" when discussing tables and operations. Follow the heading with
which data or actions change.

Before review, reread the document as connected English prose. Check that its
main point is clear without reading every detail.

## Terms and names

Use the product glossary's English terms in business requirements. Preserve names
in technical documents and machine contracts, including English example phrases
and names of English grammatical forms.

Build natural English sentences around retained technical terms. Do not reproduce
the source language's word order mechanically.

## Requirement wording

Replace "the system can" with concrete behavior or a user right. Replace vague
words such as "suitable", "high-quality" and "convenient" with explicit criteria.
Use requirements.md for REQ structure when applicable.
